The Positives & Negatives of DnD
Positives Negatives
Fun with friends Scheduling Hell
Rolling Dice Rolling Dice
Creating Characters & Developing them Characters can die and thus put you back at square one
Can be Unforgettable and Memorable Can have more bad moments than good moments
Easy to learn and make a character Players may be too attached to their characters
The Dungeon Master may be creative and unique with what they provide the players. The Dungeon Master might be inexperienced or unprepared, perhaps even uncaring for their players characters.
Fight's can be enjoyable and intriguing. Fights can lose their charm over time if nothing unique is provided
Reinforcement of friendship and social bonds Disagreements over other players actions or the DMs decisions.
DnD is easy to introduce newer players into. DnD burnout is real.
The story can be interesting and characters lores may be taken into consideration. Sometimes the narrative can become predictable or repetitive. The Lore for said characters might not actually be brought up.
Can have a lot done in one session. Said sessions usually last several hours, so if people have limited time, it may cause some scheduling issues.
Visual items can be used to enhance the TTRPG experience, minis, battleboards/maps, etc. It costs money to obtain said visuals.
Can be played in person or online. There are limitations to playing online and in person, either by expenses or certain websites and or platforms cost money to use.

Personal Opinion

Ultimately, there's a lot of goods and bads, but at the end of the day peopl come together depending on their freetime to sit down for likely the remainder of their free time or more to play a session with their characters alongside their friends, and whilst there no DnD session is perfect either by the fault of the players, system, or dungeon master, more often than not it brings good memories. At least for me, and if maybe you if you've played a campaign before.
